Common questions

The scan says someone is idle but they use Jira every day.

They probably only read boards and dashboards. Seat Saver measures work-item activity (assignee, reporter, creator, worklog) because Jira exposes no last-login to apps. Click Ignore on that user, or raise the idle threshold in Settings.

Bots and integrations show as idle.

They are flagged "likely service account". Ignore them; the headline updates instantly and they stay ignored on future scans.

The cost is wrong.

Enter what you actually pay per user per month in Settings (check your Atlassian bill). The default is a typical Jira Standard price.

Can the app deactivate users?

No, by design. It is read-only.
