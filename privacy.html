Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 September 2026.

Summary

Seat Saver is a read-only Atlassian Forge app. It runs entirely on Atlassian-hosted infrastructure ("Runs on Atlassian"). No data leaves your Atlassian tenant. We operate no servers, databases or analytics that receive your data.

Data the app reads

  • Jira user directory entries: account ID, display name, account type, active flag, application roles.
  • Application role seat counts.
  • For each licensed user, the timestamp of their most recent work item activity (via JQL). Issue content is not read or stored.

Data the app stores

Stored only in Forge Key-Value Storage inside your own Atlassian tenant, scoped to your installation: your settings (idle threshold, seat price, currency, ignored account IDs), scan results (account ID, application role keys, last-activity timestamp, computed idle days and cost, and a derived "likely service account" flag) and monthly totals. No names, email addresses or other personal data are stored. Display names are looked up from Jira each time the report is viewed and are never persisted.

Data leaving Atlassian

None. The app declares no external domains and makes no network requests outside Atlassian.

Writes to Jira

None. All permission scopes are read-only. The app cannot deactivate users or change licenses.

Retention and deletion

Scan data is overwritten on each scan. When the app is uninstalled, Atlassian deletes its storage after the standard Forge retention window (28 days).

Who can see the data

Only Jira administrators of your site, via the app's admin page.

Sub-processors

None beyond Atlassian's Forge platform.
